Research Insights

Answers to the six dataset research questions, computed from the full dataset.

Q1 — Most common apartment type

By room count across all listings
Finding: 2-room apartments are most common (28%), then 1-room (23%). Studios are 16%.

Q2 — Metro proximity and price

Median price (M RUB) by walk time to metro
Finding: Apartments within 5 min of metro cost 22% more than 6–15 min (13.4M vs 11.0M).

Q3 — Renovation and price

Median price (M RUB) by renovation level
Finding: Designer renovation is 9× more expensive than cosmetic (72M vs 8.2M). Largest jump is European to Designer (+270%).

Q4 — Moscow vs Oblast

Median price by region
Finding: Moscow costs 2.4× more than Oblast (15.9M vs 6.5M). Oblast = 29% of dataset.

Q5 — Floor preferences

Listing count by floor range
Finding: Low floors (2–5) most common (32%). Ground and top floors least common (7% each).

Q6 — Key price-driving factors

MCDA criterion weights derived from analysis
Finding: Price and metro proximity account for 55% of decision weight. Renovation, size and infrastructure share the remaining 45%.
